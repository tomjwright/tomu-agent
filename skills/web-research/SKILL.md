---
name: web-research
description: Research a question on the web, such as news, reviews or comparing options.
---
When the user wants something looked up or researched:

1. Call search_web with a short, specific query.
2. If the results are thin or don't answer the question, search again with different words.
   Stop after three searches.
3. Answer in a few lines, using only what the results say. If they disagree, say so.
4. End with the links you used, one per line.
